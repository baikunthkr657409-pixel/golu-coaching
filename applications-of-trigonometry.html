Applications of Trigonometry

Class 10 Mathematics | Chapter 9

1. Introduction

Trigonometry is not only used for finding ratios in a triangle. It can also be used to calculate heights and distances that are difficult to measure directly.

For example, trigonometry can help us find:

  • Height of a tower
  • Height of a building
  • Distance of an object
  • Height of a tree
  • Distance across a river
  • Angle of elevation
  • Angle of depression
Main idea: Convert the real-life situation into a right-angled triangle and then use an appropriate trigonometric ratio.

2. Heights and Distances

The application of trigonometry to calculate unknown heights and distances is called the study of heights and distances.

Most problems are solved by forming a right-angled triangle.

The straight line joining the observer's eye to the object being observed is called the line of sight.

When an observer looks upward at an object, the angle made by the line of sight with the horizontal line is called the angle of elevation.

6. Important Trigonometric Ratios

Ratio Formula Usually useful for
sin θ P/H Height and Hypotenuse
cos θ B/H Distance and Hypotenuse
tan θ P/B Height and Distance
tan θ = Height / Distance
In many height-and-distance problems, tan θ is the most convenient ratio because it directly connects height and distance.

7. Choosing the Correct Ratio

First identify the sides involved in the question.

Known sides Useful ratio
Height and Distance tan θ
Height and Hypotenuse sin θ
Distance and Hypotenuse cos θ
Shortcut: If the question gives height and distance and asks for an angle, think about tan θ.

8. Solved Example — Finding Height

Question

A person stands 20 m away from a tower. The angle of elevation of the top of the tower is 45°. Find the height of the tower.

Solution

Let the height of the tower be h.

Distance = 20 m

Angle = 45°

tan 45° = h / 20

We know:

tan 45° = 1

Therefore:

1 = h / 20

h = 20 m

Answer: Height of the tower = 20 m

9. Solved Example — Finding Distance

Question

A tower is 15 m high. If the angle of elevation of its top from a point on the ground is 30°, find the distance of the point from the tower.

Solution

Height = 15 m

Distance = x

tan 30° = 15/x

Since:

tan 30° = 1/√3

Therefore:

1/√3 = 15/x

x = 15√3 m

Answer: Distance = 15√3 m

10. Solved Example — Finding Angle

Question

A pole is 10 m high and its base is 10 m away from an observer. Find the angle of elevation of the top of the pole.

Solution

tan θ = Height / Distance
tan θ = 10/10 = 1

We know:

tan 45° = 1

Therefore, θ = 45°

12. Solved Example — Angle of Depression

Question

From the top of a 20 m high building, the angle of depression of an object on the ground is 45°. Find the distance of the object from the building.

Solution

Height = 20 m

Angle of depression = 45°

Corresponding angle of elevation = 45°

tan 45° = 20/x
1 = 20/x

Therefore:

x = 20 m

Answer: Distance = 20 m

14. Step-by-Step Method

  1. Read the question carefully.
  2. Draw a rough diagram.
  3. Mark the known height and distance.
  4. Mark the given angle.
  5. Identify the unknown quantity.
  6. Choose sin, cos or tan.
  7. Substitute the values.
  8. Solve the equation.
  9. Write the final answer with correct unit.
Golden Rule: Diagram → Ratio → Substitution → Calculation → Answer

15. Solved Example — Tree Problem

Question

A person standing 10 m away from a tree observes the top of the tree at an angle of elevation of 60°. Find the height of the tree.

Solution

Let height = h.

tan 60° = h/10

tan 60° = √3

√3 = h/10

h = 10√3 m

Answer: Height = 10√3 m

16. Solved Example — Shadow Problem

Question

A pole 10 m high casts a shadow of length 10 m. Find the angle of elevation of the sun.

Solution

Height = 10 m

Shadow = 10 m

tan θ = Height / Shadow
tan θ = 10/10 = 1

Since tan 45° = 1:

θ = 45°

17. Important Formula Sheet

tan θ = Height / Distance
Height = Distance × tan θ
Distance = Height / tan θ
sin θ = Height / Hypotenuse
cos θ = Distance / Hypotenuse

18. Common Mistakes

  • Do not confuse angle of elevation with angle of depression.
  • Always draw the diagram first.
  • Do not use the wrong side as the hypotenuse.
  • Keep units consistent.
  • Do not forget the degree symbol when needed.
  • Write the final answer with units such as m or cm.
  • Check whether the answer is reasonable.

8. हल किया हुआ उदाहरण — ऊँचाई

प्रश्न

एक व्यक्ति टावर से 20 m दूर खड़ा है। टावर के शीर्ष का Angle of Elevation 45° है। टावर की ऊँचाई ज्ञात कीजिए।

हल

मान लें टावर की ऊँचाई = h

tan 45° = h/20

tan 45° = 1

1 = h/20

h = 20 m

उत्तर: टावर की ऊँचाई = 20 m

9. हल किया हुआ उदाहरण — दूरी

प्रश्न

एक टावर की ऊँचाई 15 m है और किसी बिंदु से उसके शीर्ष का Angle of Elevation 30° है। टावर से उस बिंदु की दूरी ज्ञात कीजिए।

हल

tan 30° = 15/x
1/√3 = 15/x
x = 15√3 m

उत्तर: दूरी = 15√3 m

10. कोण ज्ञात करने का उदाहरण

प्रश्न

एक खंभे की ऊँचाई 10 m है और व्यक्ति खंभे से 10 m दूर है। Angle of Elevation ज्ञात कीजिए।

tan θ = 10/10 = 1

tan 45° = 1

अतः θ = 45°

15. पेड़ की ऊँचाई का उदाहरण

एक व्यक्ति पेड़ से 10 m दूर है और पेड़ के शीर्ष का Angle of Elevation 60° है। पेड़ की ऊँचाई ज्ञात कीजिए।

tan 60° = h/10

√3 = h/10

h = 10√3 m

उत्तर: पेड़ की ऊँचाई = 10√3 m

16. Shadow Problem

10 m ऊँचे खंभे की छाया की लंबाई 10 m है। सूर्य का Angle of Elevation ज्ञात कीजिए।

tan θ = ऊँचाई / छाया

tan θ = 10/10 = 1

अतः θ = 45°

17. Important Formula Sheet

tan θ = ऊँचाई / दूरी
ऊँचाई = दूरी × tan θ
दूरी = ऊँचाई / tan θ
sin θ = ऊँचाई / कर्ण
cos θ = दूरी / कर्ण
