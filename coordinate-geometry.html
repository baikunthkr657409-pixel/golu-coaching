Introduction

Coordinate Geometry is the study of geometry using coordinates on the Cartesian plane. In this chapter we learn distance, section and area formulas.

Main Topics

  • Distance Formula
  • Section Formula
  • Midpoint Formula
  • Area of a Triangle
  • Collinearity of Points

1. Distance Formula

The distance between two points A(x₁,y₁) and B(x₂,y₂) is:

d = √[(x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²]
Solved Example:

Find the distance between A(2,3) and B(5,7).

d = √[(5−2)² + (7−3)²]
= √[3² + 4²]
= √25
= 5

Answer: The distance is 5 units.

2. Section Formula

If a point P(x,y) divides the line segment joining A(x₁,y₁) and B(x₂,y₂) internally in the ratio m:n, then:

x = (mx₂ + nx₁) / (m+n)

y = (my₂ + ny₁) / (m+n)
Solved Example:

Find the point which divides A(2,3) and B(8,9) in the ratio 1:2.

x = [1(8) + 2(2)] / 3
= 12/3 = 4

y = [1(9) + 2(3)] / 3
= 15/3 = 5

Answer: The required point is (4,5).

3. Midpoint Formula

The midpoint of A(x₁,y₁) and B(x₂,y₂) is:

Midpoint = ((x₁+x₂)/2 , (y₁+y₂)/2)
Solved Example:

Find the midpoint of A(2,4) and B(6,8).

Midpoint = ((2+6)/2 , (4+8)/2)
= (8/2 , 12/2)
= (4,6)

Answer: The midpoint is (4,6).

4. Area of a Triangle

The area of a triangle whose vertices are (x₁,y₁), (x₂,y₂) and (x₃,y₃) is:

Area = 1/2 |x₁(y₂−y₃) + x₂(y₃−y₁) + x₃(y₁−y₂)|
Solved Example:

Find the area of the triangle with vertices (0,0), (4,0) and (0,3).

Area = 1/2 |0(0−3) + 4(3−0) + 0(0−0)|
= 1/2 × 12
= 6

Answer: Area = 6 square units.

5. Collinearity of Points

Three points are collinear if the area of the triangle formed by them is zero.

Area of Triangle = 0
Important Idea:

To check whether three points are collinear, use the area formula. If the area is 0, the three points lie on the same straight line.

Important Points for Exam

  • Remember the Distance Formula.
  • Remember the Section Formula carefully.
  • Midpoint is a special case of Section Formula.
  • For collinear points, area of triangle is zero.
  • Always write the formula before solving.
  • Check signs carefully while substituting coordinates.

Practice Questions

  2. Find the midpoint of (3,5) and (7,9).
  3. Find the point dividing (2,4) and (8,10) in the ratio 1:2.
  4. Find the area of the triangle whose vertices are (1,2), (4,6) and (7,2).
  5. Check whether the points (1,2), (3,4) and (5,6) are collinear.
