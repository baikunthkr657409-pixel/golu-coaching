📐 Basic Proportionality Theorem

If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle, it divides the other two sides proportionally.

DE ∥ BC ⇒ AD / DB = AE / EC

✏️ Solved Example

In triangle ABC, DE ∥ BC. If AD = 3 cm, DB = 2 cm and AE = 6 cm, find EC.

AD / DB = AE / EC

3 / 2 = 6 / EC

3 × EC = 12

EC = 4 cm

🔺 Similarity of Triangles

Two triangles are similar when their corresponding angles are equal and corresponding sides are proportional.

AA / AAA Criterion

If two corresponding angles are equal, the triangles are similar.

SSS Criterion

If corresponding sides of two triangles are proportional, the triangles are similar.

SAS Criterion

If one angle is equal and the corresponding sides including that angle are proportional, the triangles are similar.

✏️ Solved Example

Two triangles have sides 3, 4, 5 and 6, 8, 10. Check whether they are similar.

3 / 6 = 4 / 8 = 5 / 10

1 / 2 = 1 / 2 = 1 / 2

Therefore, the triangles are similar by SSS criterion.

📏 Corresponding Sides

If two triangles are similar, their corresponding sides are proportional.

AB / DE = BC / EF = AC / DF

✏️ Solved Example

If two similar triangles have corresponding sides 6 cm and 9 cm, and another side of the smaller triangle is 8 cm, find the corresponding side of the larger triangle.

6 / 9 = 8 / x

6x = 72

x = 12 cm

📊 Areas of Similar Triangles

The ratio of the areas of two similar triangles is equal to the square of the ratio of their corresponding sides.

Area₁ / Area₂ = (Corresponding side₁ / Corresponding side₂)²

✏️ Solved Example

The corresponding sides of two similar triangles are 4 cm and 6 cm. Find the ratio of their areas.

Area₁ / Area₂ = (4 / 6)²

= (2 / 3)²

= 4 / 9

Therefore, Area₁ : Area₂ = 4 : 9

📐 Pythagoras Theorem

In a right-angled triangle, the square of the hypotenuse is equal to the sum of the squares of the other two sides.

Hypotenuse² = Base² + Perpendicular²

c² = a² + b²

✏️ Solved Example

A right-angled triangle has base 6 cm and perpendicular 8 cm. Find the hypotenuse.

c² = 6² + 8²

c² = 36 + 64

c² = 100

c = 10 cm

🔄 Converse of Pythagoras Theorem

If the square of the largest side of a triangle is equal to the sum of the squares of the other two sides, then the triangle is right-angled.

If c² = a² + b², then the triangle is right-angled.

✏️ Solved Example

A triangle has sides 5 cm, 12 cm and 13 cm. Check whether it is a right-angled triangle.

13² = 5² + 12²

169 = 25 + 144

169 = 169

Therefore, the triangle is right-angled.
