🧮 Important Formulas

Quadratic Polynomial

For ax² + bx + c:

Sum of zeroes = -b/a
Product of zeroes = c/a

Cubic Polynomial

For ax³ + bx² + cx + d:

Sum of zeroes = -b/a

✏️ Example

Find the zeroes of:

x² - 5x + 6

Factorisation:

x² - 5x + 6 = (x - 2)(x - 3)

Therefore, the zeroes are:

x = 2 and x = 3

🎯 Exam Important Points

⭐ Zeroes of a polynomial are the values of x for which p(x) = 0.
⭐ A quadratic polynomial has at most two zeroes.
⭐ A cubic polynomial has at most three zeroes.
⭐ Questions based on the relationship between zeroes and coefficients are important for exams.
