Introduction to Trigonometry

Class 10 Mathematics | Chapter 8

1. Introduction

Trigonometry is an important branch of Mathematics that deals with the relationship between the angles and sides of a triangle.

The word Trigonometry comes from Greek words meaning measurement of triangles.

Class 10 Tip: Learn the basic ratios, standard values and identities carefully. These are very important for solving numerical questions.

2. Right-Angled Triangle

A triangle having one angle equal to 90° is called a right-angled triangle.

Important Parts

  • Hypotenuse: Side opposite the 90° angle. It is the longest side.
  • Perpendicular: Side opposite the selected angle.
  • Base: The remaining side adjacent to the selected angle.
Hypotenuse = Longest side of a right-angled triangle
For a selected angle θ, always identify the perpendicular, base and hypotenuse correctly before applying a trigonometric ratio.

3. Trigonometric Ratios

For an acute angle θ in a right-angled triangle, six trigonometric ratios are defined.

Ratio Formula Meaning
sin θ Perpendicular / Hypotenuse P / H
cos θ Base / Hypotenuse B / H
tan θ Perpendicular / Base P / B
cosec θ Hypotenuse / Perpendicular H / P
sec θ Hypotenuse / Base H / B
cot θ Base / Perpendicular B / P
sin θ = P/H

cos θ = B/H

tan θ = P/B

cosec θ = H/P

sec θ = H/B

cot θ = B/P

4. Easy Way to Remember Ratios

SOH → Sin = Opposite / Hypotenuse

CAH → Cos = Adjacent / Hypotenuse

TOA → Tan = Opposite / Adjacent

Here:

  • Opposite = Perpendicular
  • Adjacent = Base
  • Hypotenuse = Longest side
Remember: SOH – CAH – TOA

5. Reciprocal Relationships

Some trigonometric ratios are reciprocals of each other.

cosec θ = 1 / sin θ

sec θ = 1 / cos θ

cot θ = 1 / tan θ

Therefore:

sin θ × cosec θ = 1

cos θ × sec θ = 1

tan θ × cot θ = 1

6. Relation Between tan, sin and cos

tan θ = sin θ / cos θ
cot θ = cos θ / sin θ

Example

If sin θ = 3/5 and cos θ = 4/5, find tan θ.

tan θ = sin θ / cos θ

= (3/5) ÷ (4/5)

= 3/4

Answer: tan θ = 3/4

7. Standard Angles

The standard angles commonly used in Class 10 are:

0° 30° 45° 60° 90°
θ 0° 30° 45° 60° 90°
sin θ 0 1/2 1/√2 √3/2 1
cos θ 1 √3/2 1/√2 1/2 0
tan θ 0 1/√3 1 √3 Not defined

cot θ Not defined √3 1 1/√3 0

8. How to Remember sin Values

sin θ = √(0, 1, 2, 3, 4) / 2

For angles:

Angle Value
0° √0/2 = 0
30° √1/2 = 1/2
45° √2/2 = 1/√2
60° √3/2
90° √4/2 = 1

9. Important Trigonometric Identities

The following identities are very important for Class 10 examinations.

sin² θ + cos² θ = 1
1 + tan² θ = sec² θ
1 + cot² θ = cosec² θ

Other useful forms

1 − sin² θ = cos² θ

1 − cos² θ = sin² θ

sec² θ − tan² θ = 1

cosec² θ − cot² θ = 1

10. Identity Verification

To prove an identity, usually start from one side and simplify it until it becomes equal to the other side.

Example

Prove:

1 + tan² θ = sec² θ

We know:

sin² θ + cos² θ = 1

Divide both sides by cos² θ:

sin² θ/cos² θ + 1 = 1/cos² θ

Therefore:

tan² θ + 1 = sec² θ

Hence proved.

11. Finding Other Ratios When One Ratio is Given

Example 1

If sin θ = 3/5, find cos θ and tan θ.

We know:

sin² θ + cos² θ = 1

(3/5)² + cos² θ = 1

9/25 + cos² θ = 1

cos² θ = 16/25

cos θ = 4/5

Now:

tan θ = sin θ / cos θ

= (3/5)/(4/5)

= 3/4

Answer: cos θ = 4/5, tan θ = 3/4

Example 2

If tan θ = 3/4, find sin θ and cos θ.

Take:

Perpendicular = 3
Base = 4

Using Pythagoras:

Hypotenuse = √(3² + 4²) = 5

Therefore:

sin θ = 3/5

cos θ = 4/5

12. Pythagoras Theorem and Trigonometry

For a right-angled triangle:

Hypotenuse² = Perpendicular² + Base²

This theorem is frequently used while finding trigonometric ratios.

Example

A right triangle has perpendicular = 6 cm and base = 8 cm. Find the hypotenuse.

H² = 6² + 8²

H² = 36 + 64 = 100

H = 10 cm

Answer: Hypotenuse = 10 cm

13. Solved Numerical Questions

Question 1

Find sin 30° + cos 60°.

sin 30° = 1/2

cos 60° = 1/2

Therefore:

1/2 + 1/2 = 1

Answer = 1

Question 2

Find tan 45° + sin 90°.

tan 45° = 1

sin 90° = 1

1 + 1 = 2

Answer = 2

Question 3

Find sec 60°.

We know:

cos 60° = 1/2

Therefore:

sec 60° = 1/cos 60° = 1/(1/2) = 2

Answer = 2

14. Important Points for Exam

  • Hypotenuse is always opposite the 90° angle.
  • Hypotenuse is always the longest side.
  • sin θ = Perpendicular/Hypotenuse.
  • cos θ = Base/Hypotenuse.
  • tan θ = Perpendicular/Base.
  • cosec θ is reciprocal of sin θ.
  • sec θ is reciprocal of cos θ.
  • cot θ is reciprocal of tan θ.
  • tan θ = sin θ/cos θ.
  • cot θ = cos θ/sin θ.
  • Learn all standard angle values.
  • Remember the three major identities.

15. Common Mistakes

  • Do not confuse base and perpendicular.
  • Do not take the wrong side as the hypotenuse.
  • Remember that tan 90° is not defined.
  • Remember that cot 90° = 0.
  • Do not forget squares in identities such as sin² θ.
  • While proving identities, simplify carefully.

2. समकोण त्रिभुज

जिस त्रिभुज का एक कोण 90° होता है उसे समकोण त्रिभुज कहा जाता है।

मुख्य भाग

  • कर्ण: 90° कोण के सामने वाली भुजा। यह सबसे लंबी होती है।
  • लंब: चुने गए कोण के सामने वाली भुजा।
  • आधार: चुने गए कोण के पास वाली दूसरी भुजा।
